AI Prioritization Pipeline

How our system automatically routes your complaints for fastest resolution.

1. Image Detection

YOLOv8 scans the uploaded image to verify the defect (e.g. 95% confident it's a pothole).

2. Text Analysis

DistilBERT analyzes complaint description for urgency keywords and classifies intent.

3. Contextual Data

We look at map location (main road vs alley) and check for duplicate reports nearby.

4. Priority Scoring

XGBoost generates a final severity score: Critical, High, Medium, or Low.
